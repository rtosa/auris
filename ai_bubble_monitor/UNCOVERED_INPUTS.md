# Uncovered Scenario Inputs and Source Audit

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-22
**Rule:** A proxy is kept separate from a direct measurement. Missing disclosures remain unavailable; they are not filled with zero.

## Live gaps

The scenario catalog includes direct Fed liquidity measures. SEC release scanning records explicitly stated AI-business revenue run rates and Data Center revenue; comparable growth is now scored only when an issuer has at least two dated observations and at least half of the configured cohort is covered. Current evidence is sufficient for one issuer in the NVDA/AMD Data Center proxy cohort, but not for AI monetization. Fiscal.ai Street Consensus supplies dated current-calendar-year snapshots for eight of Auris's 17 companies; point-in-time revisions now enter two scenario components as an uncalibrated research signal, with Zacks attribution retained in the dashboard.

Raw data may be fresh while a normalized score is unavailable. CPI, unemployment, and bank lending tightening initially failed normalization because their monthly or quarterly history could not satisfy the generic 60-observation minimum. The low-frequency minimums are now set to 48 months and 16 quarters. Live recalculation succeeded: all three now have normalized values, and MacroCrisis coverage rose to 90% and CreditCrunch coverage to 85%.

## Source audit

| Missing or weak input | Public source found | What it measures | Coverage and limitations | Recommended treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI earnings and guidance | Company 8-K/6-K earnings releases (often Exhibit 99.1) from [SEC EDGAR](https://data.sec.gov/) and Fiscal.ai/Zacks dated estimates | Analyst EPS/revenue revisions and explicit AI business / compute revenue evidence | Analyst revisions are now period-matched and scored directionally. The score is an uncalibrated proxy; reported AI revenue remains unscored until comparable growth is available. SEC evidence retains the quote, accession, publication date, and URL. | Keep the narrow revision proxy with explicit attribution and methodology notes; require prospective validation before describing it as predictive. |
| Compute demand | NVIDIA and AMD Data Center / AI compute revenue in issuer earnings releases filed with [SEC EDGAR](https://data.sec.gov/) | Revenue growth of a limited compute-supplier cohort | A comparable, same-issuer revenue growth proxy is now scored if there are repeated dated observations for at least half of the configured NVDA/AMD cohort. Data Center revenue includes non-AI workloads and is not utilization, capacity demand, or total compute demand. Cloud revenue/RPO remains a broad unscored proxy. | Label it as reported Data Center revenue growth; pursue utilization or workload-specific metrics before calling it compute demand. |
| AI monetization | SEC issuer earnings releases | Directly attributed AI revenue or run-rate claims | Explicit AI business values (for example, AWS AI business annualized run rate) preserve units so annualized run rates are not mixed with reported-quarter revenue. The current collection lacks repeated comparable observations across at least half of the tracked issuers. No amount is inferred from total cloud revenue or RPO. | Keep the score unavailable until comparable dated histories exist; continue collecting explicit claims. |
| Earnings revisions | [Fiscal.ai Street Consensus](https://docs.fiscal.ai/docs/api-reference) (Zacks data) | Dated consensus EPS and revenue estimates and their revisions | 11,216 dated observations cover 8/17 companies as of 2026-11-22, with history from 2026-01-02 through 2026-11-20. Revisions now feed scenario components, with same-period matching and 8-company scope. The +/-10% mapping is an uncalibrated directional heuristic; free-plan and Zacks usage terms still apply. | Keep it visibly labeled as uncalibrated; build longer prospective history and outcomes before evaluating predictive accuracy. |
| Private-credit stress | [Proskauer Private Credit Default Index releases](https://www.proskauer.com/release/proskauers-private-credit-default-index-reveals-rate-of-241-for-q3-2026-pr) | Quarterly US senior-secured/unitranche default headline rate | Collector now reads linked official release pages and records quarter-end observations with source URLs. HTML is not a documented API, full sector tables are client-only, and the headline index is not AI-specific. Normalization requires at least four observations; it is excluded from scenarios and confidence scores. | Keep as separate market context. Monitor parser health and do not infer missing quarters. |
| Refinancing risk | Standard SEC XBRL facts such as `LongTermDebtMaturitiesRepaymentsOfPrincipalInNextTwelveMonths` and issuer current-debt facts | Principal due within 12 months / total debt | Exact maturity schedules are used where available. For matching periods without a maturity schedule, current debt is now a fallback proxy at lower source confidence; the component input labels show when it is used. Fallback current debt can overstate or understate scheduled principal repayments. | Keep both provenance and confidence visible; expand issuer-specific inline XBRL parsing for exact maturity schedules. |
| Purchase obligations | [SEC company facts and filing-level Inline XBRL](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) | Unrecorded purchase obligations / assets | SEC's aggregate Company Facts API covers non-custom taxonomy facts applying to the whole entity; customized issuer tags require per-filing handling. Generic capex guidance is not an equivalent contractual obligation and is not used to fill gaps. | Keep missing values unavailable; add issuer-specific tag mappings only after validating each filing and period. |
| Customer concentration and commitments | SEC 10-Q/10-K/20-F inline XBRL customer dimensions and issuer notes; standard purchase-obligation fact | Highest explicitly disclosed customer share of revenue; contractual purchase obligations | The parser now scans latest filings across all 17 issuers for the standardized revenue concentration tag, while retaining issuer-specific definitions where available. Each filing is marked scanned even when no matching disclosure exists, so absence is not repeatedly fetched or treated as zero. Previously validated coverage was 7/17; the new broader scan may add observations, which require live SEC collection to establish actual coverage. Values remain outside scoring. | Recheck cross-group coverage after SEC collection; keep purchase obligations separate. |
| Broad USD/direct liquidity | FRED [reserve balances](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/WRESBAL), [Treasury General Account](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/WTREGEN), [overnight reverse repo](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/RRPONTSYD), and [broad USD](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DTWEXBGS) | Weekly bank reserves, TGA, daily RRP usage, plus a separate trade-weighted dollar proxy | Three direct liquidity series are added to the catalog and Macro Crisis / Current Stress scoring. Higher reserves are favorable; higher TGA and RRP are risk-oriented. Broad USD remains separately labeled and capped as a proxy. The direct inputs are combined as normalized component inputs rather than advertised as a single official Fed liquidity index. | Monitor history normalization, staleness and score contribution after first live collection; do not interpret the component as a published Fed index. |
