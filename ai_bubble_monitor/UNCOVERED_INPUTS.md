# Uncovered Scenario Inputs and Source Audit

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-22
**Rule:** A proxy is kept separate from a direct measurement. Missing disclosures remain unavailable; they are not filled with zero.

## Live gaps

The latest local scenario catalog has no inputs for AI earnings guidance, compute demand, AI monetization, earnings revisions, and customer concentration. CreditCrunch has company-balance-sheet proxies and a quarterly bank-lending proxy, but not an AI-specific default or refinancing series. MacroCrisis has energy and activity data; its broad-dollar observation is stale.

Raw data may be fresh while a normalized score is unavailable. CPI, unemployment, and bank lending tightening initially failed normalization because their monthly or quarterly history could not satisfy the generic 60-observation minimum. The low-frequency minimums are now set to 48 months and 16 quarters. Live recalculation succeeded: all three now have normalized values, and MacroCrisis coverage rose to 90% and CreditCrunch coverage to 85%.

## Source audit

| Missing or weak input | Public source found | What it measures | Coverage and limitations | Recommended treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI earnings and guidance | Company 8-K/6-K earnings releases (often Exhibit 99.1), 10-Q and 10-K filings from [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) | Issuer-reported results, outlook and capex guidance | Incremental scanner covers up to six recent 8-K/6-K filings per issuer, at most two in each collection cycle; it prefers an Exhibit 99.1 HTML release and records explicit forward-looking numeric CAPEX point estimates or ranges with the exact sentence, accession, release date, and URL. Same-company, same-year revisions are compared. A live checked Amazon Q3 2026 release says it expects 2026 cash CAPEX of approximately $220B; the parser records $220B. Coverage and false-positive review are still incomplete; qualitative guidance, AI revenue, and issuer-specific AI attribution remain unavailable. | Treat extracted events as source-linked evidence only; do not feed them into scenario scores until issuer coverage, false-positive review, and comparable-period history are validated. |
| Compute demand | Segment revenue and backlog in issuer 10-Q/10-K XBRL filings; [SEC Financial Statement and Notes data](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/financial-statement-and-notes-data-sets) is a public bulk alternative | AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle Cloud revenue; company-wide remaining performance obligations (RPO) | Four-year inline XBRL backfill is complete and idempotent. Cloud-revenue YoY samples: AWS 13 (12.16–39.32%), Google Cloud 10 (22.47–102.61%), Oracle Cloud 13 (21.32–61.52%), Azure 1 (48.16%). Older Azure dollar values are not reported in the 2025 filing; Google's nested dimensions and Oracle's historical dimension alias are handled. These are still broad proxies, not direct AI compute demand. | Keep proxy outside scoring: sample sizes and disclosure histories differ too much for a calibrated group score. |
| AI monetization | SEC segment revenue and RPO disclosures | Cloud or AI-related backlog and revenue trends | Current SEC filings provide comparable company-wide RPO tags for Amazon, Microsoft, Alphabet, and Oracle; RPO is contract backlog, not AI-specific monetization. The four-year backfill now provides 7–10 YoY periods per issuer, but observed changes include very large step changes and do not represent AI-specific revenue. | Keep RPO and cloud revenue labeled as broad proxies; do not score until periods and issuer coverage are comparable and changes are understood. |
| Earnings revisions | [Alpha Vantage EARNINGS_ESTIMATES](https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/#earnings-estimates) and [FMP Financial Estimates](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/developer/docs/stable/financial-estimates) | Annual/quarterly EPS and revenue consensus, analyst count | Alpha Vantage documents estimates and revision history, but its [terms](https://www.alphavantage.co/terms_of_service/) classify individual investment research/testing/monitoring as commercial use. FMP provides forward estimates but confirms it has no point-in-time as-of snapshots on any plan; the endpoint is marked limited access and the free plan excludes premium endpoints. These do not currently offer a suitable no-cost, reusable historical revision series. | Keep analyst revisions unavailable. Revisit only if access rights and dated consensus snapshots become suitable. Issuer guidance remains a separate metric. |
| Private-credit stress | [Proskauer Private Credit Default Index releases](https://www.proskauer.com/release/proskauers-private-credit-default-index-reveals-rate-of-241-for-q3-2026-pr); Federal Reserve [SLOOS](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DRTSCILM) and [business-loan delinquencies](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DRBLACBS) | Proskauer's quarterly US senior-secured/unitranche headline default rate; broad bank lending and delinquency measures | Public quarterly articles are available for Q4 2025 through Q3 2026: 2.46%, 2.73%, 2.51%, and 2.41%. The reported loan count/original-principal sample also changed each quarter (691/$144.5B to 746/$205.7B); the full report is client-only. The official Browse Insights page is HTML, not a documented API, and returned HTTP 500 on an immediate repeat fetch during source validation. SLOOS and bank-loan delinquencies remain broad banking proxies. | Keep the series out of scoring and don't add a scheduled scraper until the official site provides a stable, repeatable public listing or feed. Never infer missing quarters. |
| Refinancing risk | Standard SEC XBRL facts such as `LongTermDebtMaturitiesRepaymentsOfPrincipalInNextTwelveMonths` | Principal due within 12 months | In a read-only current Company Facts sample, 13/17 companies had the tag and 11/17 had a reporting period within 550 days. Coverage varies by group; absent facts are not assumed to mean zero debt due. | Implemented in Credit Crunch: maturity / total debt, with matching-period facts and a 550-day freshness gate. |
| Customer concentration and commitments | SEC 10-Q/10-K/20-F inline XBRL customer dimensions and issuer notes; standard purchase-obligation fact | Highest explicitly disclosed customer share of revenue; contractual purchase obligations | A filing-level parser now extracts each issuer's largest disclosed revenue concentration, using customer and revenue benchmark dimensions; receivables concentration is excluded. Current coverage is 7/17: all four neoclouds (CRWV, NBIS, IREN, APLD), plus DLR, MU, and TSM. Current shares range from 11% to 56%. Absence is unavailable, never treated as zero. These values are exposed with filing provenance but remain outside scoring because coverage is uneven across the monitored groups. | Expand and evaluate cross-group coverage before scoring; keep purchase obligations as a separate Credit Crunch proxy. |
| Broad USD/liquidity | FRED [Nominal Broad U.S. Dollar Index](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DTWEXBGS) | Trade-weighted dollar movement against a broad group of U.S. trading partners | The configured `DTWEXBGS` is the active goods-and-services index, and FRED identifies it as the successor to the retired goods-only index. FRED showed its latest observation as 2026-11-13 and next release as 2026-11-23 during this review; the app's 7-day threshold therefore marked it stale on Nov 22. The advanced-economies index is a narrower basket, not a like-for-like replacement. Dollar strength remains only a liquidity proxy. | Keep `DTWEXBGS`; do not swap to a narrower basket. Treat the temporary stale status as a release-cadence issue and continue to label direct liquidity as unavailable. |
